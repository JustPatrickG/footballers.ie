# Player photos

Drop image files here and reference them in `data/manual/players.csv`:

| column | example |
|---|---|
| `photo` | `photos/evan-ferguson.jpg` |
| `photo_credit` | `© Sportsfile` |

A full `https://` URL also works if the image is hosted elsewhere with permission.

Square images around 400×400 look best. If `photo` is blank, the site shows the
player's initials instead — that's the default and it looks fine.

## Where photos can legitimately come from

- **The player or their club**, with permission (clubs will often say yes to a
  small site that credits them)
- **Wikimedia Commons** — genuinely free, but check each image's licence and
  credit it as that licence requires
- **A photographer you've paid or agreed terms with** — LOI has plenty of
  freelancers; some will trade photos for a credit and a link
- **Your own photos**

Press-agency photos (Inpho, Sportsfile, Getty, PA) that appear on other football
sites are licensed *to those sites*. Copying them across is the kind of thing
agencies invoice for, so don't lift them — ask, or use one of the routes above.
Always fill in `photo_credit`.
